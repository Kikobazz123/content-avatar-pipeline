I built nine tools in a few weeks and made exactly zero naira.

That is not a failure post. It is the honest starting position of a backend developer who decided to stop chasing clients.

For months the pattern was the same. Send the proposal. Wait. Hope somebody picks me. Then I asked a different question: what if I became the thing companies actually need?

So I gave myself one challenge. Learn AI automation, build a real business, and document every day of it — the wins and the mistakes both.

Africa is untouched technical ground. Bring real capability here and you do not compete, you stand out.

Day one starts today. I build AI automations for Nigerian businesses.
